\begin{theorem*}[Closed Sets in a Topological Space]
Let \((X,\tau)\) be a topological space.  The closed subsets of \(X\) include
\(X\) and \(\emptyset\), are closed under arbitrary intersections, and are
closed under finite unions.
\end{theorem*}
